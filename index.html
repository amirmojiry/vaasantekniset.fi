About Vaasa Tekniset

International expertise, based in Vaasa.

We are an international technical group residing in Vaasa. Our aim is to make it easier for customers to access reliable technical expertise through one coordinated partner.

Our network includes professionals in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, ICT, telecommunications, software, ERP and related certified technical fields. We are continuing to strengthen the team, particularly in automation, IT and HVAC systems.

For larger projects, our model is to combine our core team with reputable specialist companies and certified professionals, allowing us to scale capability without unnecessary overhead.

What we focus on Qualified work · transparent pricing · practical coordination · long-term technical value
